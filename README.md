# Nexus Resilience AI 

> **Predict. Understand. Act. Before Risk Becomes Loss.**

**Nexus Resilience AI** is an agentic AI-powered operational resilience platform designed to help organizations identify emerging operational risk, understand why it is occurring, prioritize facilities, and support timely intervention.

The platform combines **predictive analytics, BigQuery intelligence, Gemini-powered reasoning, Google Agent Development Kit (ADK), and executive visualization** into a unified decision-support workflow.

Rather than presenting isolated dashboards or predictions, Nexus Resilience AI connects operational data to executive questions:

- What is happening?
- Which facility requires attention first?
- What evidence supports that priority?
- What is likely to happen?
- Why is the risk occurring?
- What should decision-makers consider doing next?

## Project Overview

The challenge is not simply collecting this data.
